The tiny rule

Ask: Who or what gets the action? That word is often the accusative object. Masculine der changes to den. Feminine die and neuter das stay the same. ✨

With “a”

Masculine ein becomes einen. Feminine eine and neuter ein stay the same. 🧠

💛 Getting one wrong is part of learning. Try again as often as you like.
