Tiny German Quest

Little lessons for big German grammar. Pick a quest and go one cosy step at a time. 🌈

🌱 Quest 1 Nominative Adventure Meet the subject — who or what is doing the action. Der Hund schläft. → 🎯 Quest 2 Akkusativ Adventure Find the direct object — what gets the action. Ich sehe den Hund. →

Tip: Start with the Nominative — once you can spot the subject, the Akkusativ is much easier to find.
