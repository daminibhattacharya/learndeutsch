Nominative Adventure

Meet the subject of a sentence: the person or thing doing the action. One tiny step at a time. 🌱

Ask: “Who is doing it?” 🕵️

The answer is usually the subject. In German, the subject is in the nominative.

Meet the article crew 🎨

der Mann · die Frau · das Kind · die Eltern

For now, learn each noun together with its article. The subject can appear at the start of a sentence, even when another word comes after it.

When you mean “a” 🌸

Ein Vater kauft einen Pullover. A father buys a sweater.

Eine Mutter kauft eine Erdbeere. A mother buys a strawberry.

Ein Kind braucht ein Bett. A child needs a bed.

Masculine and neuter use ein. Feminine uses eine. ✨

💛 Mistakes are clues. Try again as often as you like.
